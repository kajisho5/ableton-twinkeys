## New
- Control Live on both Macs from an iPhone or iPad with the new app TwinKeys Remote: scenes and clips (with the time left on playing clips), play and stop, tempo, the mixer (volume, pan, mute, solo), your cue keys and All Notes Off. The iPhone connects to the main Mac, which sends the same action to the backup.
- To connect, an iPhone needs the same pairing code and is allowed once on the Mac (check the 4 digits shown on both).
- New in Settings: Live. Install into Live adds a small Live part (TwinKeysBridge). It accepts commands only from the same Mac.
- Show safety on the iPhone: Stop and Stop all clips need a long press, and the show lock freezes tempo and the mixer.

## Fixed
- When TwinKeys on the backup was reopened, cues still waiting for an answer were sent again and could play twice.
- When both Macs reconnected at almost the same moment, the cue count could differ between them.
- Packets arriving out of order could make a working link look lost.
- A recorded and replayed old message could bring old cues into Live.
- When a key was stopped by macOS (for example during password entry), the cue kept waiting for confirmation.
- Connect by IP did not accept an address with a port or an IPv6 address.
- After pulling and plugging the cable back in, the link sometimes stayed off the cable.
- While connected, the Mac could sleep or slow TwinKeys down to save power.
- Turning automatic updates off and straight back on could delete the download.

## Notes
- iPhone connections are off at first. Turn them on in Settings under iPhone and iPad (TwinKeys Remote).
- TwinKeys Remote is on TestFlight for now.
- Put 2.0.0 on both Macs. Cues still reach a 1.0.x Mac, but counts and other details may not match.
- Updates are automatic from 1.0.0 on. The new version is put in place when you quit TwinKeys.
