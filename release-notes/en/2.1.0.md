## New
- GO screen on the iPhone: Live's scene list is the script. The big GO launches the scene selected in Live on both Macs and moves on. Back and Next only choose; they never play. Works with the show lock on.
- Set check: when the two Macs have different sets open (track or scene counts or names differ), a yellow warning appears on the Mac and the iPhone, with the rows that differ. Nothing is changed automatically.
- Whether the backup's Live answers is shown in the iPhone's top bar and on the Mac.
- Send A and Send B in the iPhone mixer (when the set has return tracks).
- Fader, pan, tempo, mute and solo changes made while the link to the backup was down are sent when it is back (clip and scene launches are never sent late).
- What each iPhone did (launches, stops, cues, All Notes Off, show lock) is written to the Mac's log.

## Fixed
- When two iPhones asked to connect at almost the same moment, clicking Allow on the older prompt could allow the other iPhone.
- Approval prompts could pop up again and again and take focus from Live. Now one device is asked at a time, the question ends after 60 s, and when TwinKeys is in the background only the Dock icon bounces.
- The show lock was only enforced on the iPhone. The Mac now also refuses tempo and mixer changes while it is on.
- The show lock and the long-press stops could be bypassed with one action in the iPhone's settings or with VoiceOver.
- Swiping the mixer sideways could jump a track's volume to the finger.
- The iPhone stayed "connected" after Wi-Fi dropped, and retried every 3 s forever with a wrong pairing code.
- Dead iPhone connections piled up on the Mac; after 8, nobody could connect.
- When Live was started after TwinKeys, the link to Live could stay broken.
- Adding scenes or tracks during a show could nearly freeze Live.
- Right after a track was added, an iPhone command could launch a different clip. Commands aimed at an older layout are now refused with "tap again".
- Deleted tracks and scenes stayed on the iPhone.
