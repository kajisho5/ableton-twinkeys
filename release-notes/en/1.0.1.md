## New
- Settings > Cue Keys shows the MIDI channel that is actually used.
- Release pages now have clearer Japanese and English headings.

## Fixed
- When a cue was pressed twice quickly and the first one was lost on the network, it was not sent again and the backup stayed one cue behind with no warning.
- After a short network drop, the "Cue n since connecting" numbers could stay different on the two Macs.
- Someone on the same network replaying recorded traffic could keep the link down.
- A cue still waiting for its answer could disappear from the screen (after a reconnect, or a cue from the other Mac) instead of showing "No confirmation".
- In TwinKeys' own memo and pairing-code fields, Space and the arrow keys could not be typed (or were sent as cues).
- When TwinKeys started without its window (for example at login), cues were not sent.
- While the Add Key sheet was open, shortcuts such as ⌃⌘P (All Notes Off) did not work.
- Closing Settings while typing a memo could lose the memo.
- An automatic update could start downloading during a show (while connected). It now waits until not connected.
- While a menu was open, TwinKeys did not notice Secure Input or permissions being turned off.
- One unreadable cue key in the settings file reset all cue keys to the defaults.

## Notes
- 1.0.0 updates itself to this version. The app is replaced when TwinKeys quits.
