## New
- Switching to the backup Mac when the main Mac stops. The backup Mac shows "Make This Mac the Main", and the iPhone can switch too ("Switch to the backup", hold 1 second). When the old main comes back it only receives, so the two Macs never send keys at the same time. "Return to Main" hands the role back.
- The backup Mac reports whether each action also ran in its Live. When it ran only on the main Mac, the iPhone says so.
- When Live answers late and runs an action after all, the iPhone says "Live ran it after all (late). Do not tap again."
- When the tracks or scenes the iPhone was showing no longer match Live, the action is refused before it reaches either Live (so a different clip never plays).
- Choose what the show lock stops (tempo, mixer, clip and scene launches, stops, cue buttons). GO and All Notes Off are never stopped.
- Updating TwinKeys updates TwinKeysBridge in Live too. The app tells you when Live must be restarted.
- iPhone app (TwinKeys Remote 1.2.0): an Apple Watch app (GO, choose scene, All Notes Off); "Try with sample data" on the pairing screen to try the screens without a Mac; privacy policy and support links in Settings.

## Notes
- Update both Macs to 2.2.0. Mixed with 2.1.0 the cues still arrive, but switching to the backup and the backup's confirmation do not work.
- To switch to the backup from the iPhone, also turn on "Allow TwinKeys Remote to connect" on the backup Mac and allow the iPhone there once.
- The Apple Watch works through the iPhone: keep TwinKeys Remote open on the iPhone.
- From 1.0.0 on, TwinKeys updates itself. The new version takes over when you quit TwinKeys.
