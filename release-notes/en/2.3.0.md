## New
- A new icon: two keys side by side with one down-arrow across both. The left key is the main Mac, the right key the backup (the iPhone and Apple Watch apps share it).
- (Experimental, off by default) Takeover of Yamaha console faders (QL1, QL5, DM3, DM5, DM7, including the Compact models). When the main Mac stops, the Mac still running raises the backup's channels to the level the main's had and pulls the main's channels down. Both Macs connect to the console, and the settings made on one Mac are copied to the other. It has not been tried on a real console yet: test it before a show.

## Fixed
- A new iPhone app connected to an older Mac (2.1) was disconnected again and again. Older Macs now get messages they understand.
- When Live answered late, "ran late" could be followed by "no answer".
- Depending on the order of the answers, the backup's Live refusing an action could go unreported.
- "Return to Main" while the Macs were not linked left both Macs only receiving. It is now unavailable while they are not linked.
- With a long backup Mac name, the iPhone said "Backup Mac not found".
- After a failed switch from the iPhone, the next Mac it connected to could be asked to become the main.
- With "Mixer" not in the show-lock scope, the iPhone mixer still looked locked. Blocked actions now say "Show lock is on."
- Disconnect, or quitting the main Mac normally, showed the red "When the main Mac has stopped" banner.
- With the two Macs' clocks apart, the Mac where "Make This Mac the Main" was clicked last could fail to become the main.
- Screen updates did a little more work than needed.

## Notes
- Update both Macs to 2.3.0. The console takeover needs 2.3.0 on both Macs.
- From 1.0.0 on, TwinKeys updates itself. The new version takes over when you quit TwinKeys.
